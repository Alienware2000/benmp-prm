# 0048 — Admins charts: the four numbers together, and finance

_2026-11-24_

**Decided**: on every Admins page (all quadrants, one quadrant, one hub) the single chart of Decision 0045 is replaced by two:

1. **Members, month by month**: a grouped column chart with the four numbers (total registered, active partners, new registrations, lapsed) side by side for each of the 12 months, one fixed colour each, one axis (all are head counts), a legend, and a hover tooltip with all four values. Clicking a big value now **highlights** that number in the chart (the others soften) instead of switching the chart.
2. **Finance: amount given, month by month**: a column chart of the US dollars the churches reported (Decision 0047).

A fifth big value, **Given (USD)**, sits beside the four. Same data as before (the forms), no new route.

**Why**: the office (2026-11-24): "Two graphs on the admin page, first being a column with 4 data sets per month on there (registered, active, new and lapsed) and the second being the total amount given - the finance".

**Said no to**: a second axis for the smaller numbers (one axis; total registered dwarfs new and lapsed, which the tooltip and the tables give exactly) · stacking the four (they overlap as counts: active and lapsed are part of registered).
